## Important Findings to Preserve

- Relation selection determines which event-pair geometry is meaningful.
- All-pairs interval materialization is combinatorially unacceptable.
- Endpoint displacement is translation-invariant and path-compositional.
- Missing intermediate observations contract adjacent displacement edges by vector addition.
- Signed boundary gap is not generally path-additive.
- Translation invariance, scale invariance, and observation completeness are distinct query semantics.
- A long observed displacement does not prove missing events; ambiguity must be preserved.

## Drift Boundary

Do not turn physics analogies (`worldline`, `spacetime`, `causal future`) into canonical vocabulary without precise computational semantics and comparative evidence.

Do not infer causality from temporal precedence.

Do not treat trajectory-classification accuracy on synthetic families as evidence that trajectories must become persistent primitives.

If work resumes, prioritize tests that challenge the event-geometry hypothesis rather than continuously adding analytical features.
